ABOUT US

Your Trusted Partner for Industrial Machinery Solutions

Founded in 1985, V.S. Engineers has steadily built a legacy as one of India's most reliable and respected manufacturers of high-quality industrial machinery. With over 35+ years of rich industry experience, our commitment to innovation, precision engineering and customer satisfaction has remained at the core of everything we do.

Over the years we have successfully designed and delivered a wide spectrum of industrial machines tailored to meet diverse manufacturing requirements.

Our extensive product portfolio includes:

  • Grinding Machines – engineered for smooth and precise operations
  • Cutting Machines – built for durability and high-performance cutting tasks
  • Mixer Machines – developed for efficient blending in industrial settings
  • Incense Cone Machines – specially designed for high-speed automated production
  • Customized Industrial Solutions – tailored to specific client requirements
