What Is a Dhoop Raw Mixture Machine?

A Dhoop Raw Mixture Machine is a heavy-duty industrial mixer. It blends natural ingredients like wood powder, charcoal, herbs, gum, fragrance oils, and binders into a smooth paste or even dry blend. This final mixture is used to make dhoop sticks, cones, sambrani cups, and other incense products.

Mixing this by hand is slow, tiring, and gives uneven results. A good machine from a trusted Dhoop Raw Mixture Machine Manufacturer does the same work in minutes — with much better quality and zero physical effort.

Types of Dhoop Mixing Machines

There are mainly two types of machines used in this industry.

1. Wet Dhoop Raw Mixture Machine

This is the most common type. It mixes wet ingredients like oils, water, and binders into a soft dough. As a trusted Wet Dhoop Raw Mixture Machine Manufacturer, V.S. Engineers offers drums in multiple sizes — perfect for dhoop sticks, cones, and premium incense.

2. Dry Dhoop Raw Mixture Machine

Used for blending only dry powders. Best for masala incense and powder-based products.

Our machines are dual-purpose — they handle both wet and dry materials with equal ease.

  3. Material quality — Strong mild steel or stainless steel body.
  4. Warranty — At least 1 year warranty in writing.
  5. After-sales support — Spare parts and service nearby.
  6. Demo — Always ask for a working demo before paying.
  7. GST bill — Always take a proper invoice.

Expert Tips for Buyers

  • Always visit the factory before placing a big order.
  • Ask for client references and case studies.
